## Prompt A — «3:00 AM»

YouTube thumbnail, 16:9. Minimalist white line-art illustration on a pure black background, like a fine engraving drawn with
thin white chalk-like lines and delicate hatching, high detail, monochrome white on black. On the left 45 percent of the frame,
a large close-up of a simple stick-figure-like older man with a big bald round head drawn as a black circle with a white
outline, no hair, wide open dot eyes, a small white moustache, a worried tired expression, wearing striped pajamas, lying in bed
with the blanket pulled up to his chest, his head on a pillow, staring up in the dark. Next to his head on a nightstand, an old
twin-bell alarm clock glows in bright yellow neon light with a soft golden glow, its two hands pointing to three o'clock.
Everything else is white lines on black. On the right 55 percent of the frame, a huge two-line title in heavy condensed bold
sans-serif capitals, white letters with a strong yellow neon glow around them: first line the characters 3:00, second line the
letters AM. The title is very large, fully inside the frame, perfectly legible, and does not overlap the man. High contrast,
sharp focus on the face. No other text, no logos, no watermark, no border, no color other than white lines and yellow neon.
