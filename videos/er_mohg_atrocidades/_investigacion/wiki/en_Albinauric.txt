The '''Albinaurics''' are a [[faction]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
The albinaurics are artificial life found across the [[Lands Between]] either as a [[First-Generation Albinauric]] (including an [[Albinauric Archer|archer variant]]) or [[Second-Generation Albinauric]].

==Background==
Albinaurics are lifeforms made by human hands, forsaken to the [[Lands Between]] by the [[stars]].<ref name="Elder Albinauric">[[Elder Albinauric]]: ''"Oh, stars. Oh night. Oh...fate. Punish them all. Punish the world, entire. We speak to those who forsake us to this world that shuns our kind. Punish every living thing... Punish every living thing... Punish...every...living...thing... Punish every living thing..."''</ref> Thus, many believe them to live impure lives, untouched by the [[Erdtree]]'s [[grace]].<ref name="Albinauric Bloodclot">[[Albinauric Bloodclot]]: ''"The thick, coagulated blood of the Albinaurics. […] Albinaurics are lifeforms made by human hands. Thus, many believe them to live impure lives, untouched by the Erdtree's grace."''</ref> They are said to have been created in the [[Eternal Cities|Eternal City]] from a primordial drop of [[night]]-tinged [[dew]],<ref name="Albinauric Shield">[[Albinauric Shield]]: ''"Tall oval shield made of metal carried by young Albinaurics. The ornamentation represents the primordial drop of dew from which they are said to have been created. Boasts exceptional magic damage negation. The Albinaurics' most formidable foes were sorcerers, after all."''</ref><ref name="Silver Mirrorshield">[[Silver Mirrorshield]]: ''"Shield of radiant silver, festooned with amber and carried by Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree. The shape is said to imitate that of a sacred drop of dew, which inspired the absurd rumor that Loretta herself was an Albinauric."''</ref> and secretly harbour the ability to cast [[spells|magic]] using their innate [[arcane]]ness.<ref name="Dewkissed Herba">[[Dewkissed Herba]]: ''"A herb that grows in the false night in and around the Eternal City. […] Soaked in arcane dew, it gives off the faint glow of starlight."''</ref><ref name="Albinauric Staff">[[Albinauric Staff]]: ''"Short staff with a blue glintstone embedded. Wielded by the Albinaurics of old. The Albinaurics harbor a secret; they cast sorcery using their innate arcaneness."''</ref> All Albinaurics share a fate; their [[legs]] will one day fade, and with them, their lives.<ref name="Old Albus01">[[Old Albus]]: ''"My legs will soon fade, and with them, my life. Alas, this is the immovable fate of all Albinaurics."''</ref> This process can be seen most clearly in the bearded [[Elder Albinauric]]s that inhabit the [[Village of the Albinaurics]].

Albinaurics are viewed as impure, and are persecuted by the [[Knights of the Cuckoo]] for their defiled blood.<ref name="Albinauric Pot">[[Albinauric Pot]]: ''"<!--Enchanted by sorceries of the Cuckoos. Consumes FP. Throw at enemies to impede recovery actions using a flask of tears for a certain duration. -->The Knights of the Cuckoos do declare. Behold, thy defiled blood. Unlike any humor that flows in our grand realm."''</ref> They seek salvation in [[Miquella]]'s [[Haligtree]], which they believe to be their chosen land.<ref name"Old Albus02">[[Old Albus]]: ''"A chosen land awaits us Albinaurics. The medallion is the key that leads to the city."''</ref> Aged Albinaurics carry [[Ivory Sickle]]s in dedication to the Haligtree, despite having never entered its presence.<ref name="Ivory Sickle">[[Ivory Sickle]]: ''"Sickle fashioned from ivory. Weapon carried by aged Albinaurics. These weapons are evidence of their dedication to the Haligtree, despite never having entered its presence."''</ref>

==Characteristics==
Albinaurics have silver blood. All first-generation Albinaurics are disabled and have lost the use of their legs. The males appear as withered old men with long beards whose legs are vanishing into shadow. The female archers, who wear blue silver mail born from the same mother as themselves<ref name="Blue Silver Mail Armor">[[Blue Silver Mail Armor]]: ''"Chainmail armor crafted with blue silver. Worn by the wolf-riding Albinauric archers. Blue silver is a metal born from the same mother as the archers themselves, and provides protection from magic and frost."''</ref>, also lack the functionality of their legs, which they compensate for by riding wolfback. Nonetheless, they were feared as deadly sharpshooters.<ref name="Sharpshot">[[Sharpshot Talisman]]: ''"The Albinauric archers were in a class of their own, and their deadly long-range specialists struck fear into the hearts of men."''</ref>

Second-generation Albinaurics differ visually from the more humanoid first generation specimens, having heads that resemble those of frogs and protruding bellies.<ref name="Albinauric Ashes">[[Albinauric Ashes]]: ''"Ashen remains in which spirits yet dwell. Use to summon the spirits of two albinaurics. A strapping duo of cartwheeling spirits who wield ripple swords and spew freezing breath. Both are second-generation albinaurics, with dumpy heads that resemble those of frogs."''</ref><ref name="Dirty Chainmail">[[Dirty Chainmail]]: ''"Chainmail worn by young Albinaurics. The appearance of a protruding belly provides no benefit worthy of note."''</ref> They wield weaponry modelled after the ripples that are said to be the origin of their species,<ref name="Ripple Blade">[[Ripple Blade]]: ''"Unique weapon wielded by young Albinaurics, this sword is modelled after the ripples that are thought to be the origin of their species. Its attack power is greatly enhanced with one's arcane attribute."''</ref><ref name="Ripple Crescent Halberd">[[Ripple Crescent Halberd]]: ''"Weapon wielded by young Albinaurics. The blade is said to resemble a partial image of a ripple, and its attack power scales with one's arcane attribute."''</ref> as well as wooden clubs shaped like the [[Crescent Moon]].<ref name="Curved Club">[[Curved Club]]: ''"Hard, curved club, shaped like a crescent moon. Wielded instead of a curved sword by Albinaurics lacking dexterity. The only point of similarity between the two is the shape."''</ref><ref name="Curved Great Club">[[Curved Great Club]]: ''"Large curved club of hard wood shaped like a crescent moon. Wielded by Albinaurics lacking the dexterity demanded by a curved sword, and who prefer the added weight and destructive force."''</ref>

==Notes==
* Their natural [[Arcane]]ss raises their {{dmg|holy}} [[Holy]] defense.
* [[Sanguine Albinauric#Large|Large Sanguine Albinaurics]] at the [[Mohgwyn Palace]] turned red and sprouted [[horns]] after receiving [[Mohg]]'s accursed [[Omen]] blood and a boon from the [[Formless Mother]]. They blow into [[Curved Great Club]]s to summon Tibia's [[Giant Skeleton]]s. Others sprout piercing [[bloodthorns]] whenever they cartwheel, a power of the [[Blood Star]].
* [[Albinauric's Shack]] and [[Commander Gaius]] are found in proximity to the [[Scadutree Chalice]], suggesting its [[Sap|blessed sap]] was the amber that catalyzed the origin of their species.<ref name="Silver Mirrorshield"/>
* The [[wp:Tibia|tibia]] also known as the shinbone or shankbone, is the larger, stronger, and anterior (frontal) of the two bones in the leg below the knee. The first generation of Albinaurics lost the use of their tibias, and are unable to walk on their own.
* [[Oracle Envoy]]s, the [[Putrescent Knight]], and the [[Prince of Death]] are also legless. The nameless [[God (Ancient Dynasty)|bearded god]] of the [[Ancient Dynasty]] is depicted without legs at the [[Cocoon of the Empyrean]].
* [[Tibia Mariner]]s and the giant mummified [[maidens]] perched atop [[Chair-Crypt]]s also appear immobile.
* Albinaurics have white blood, though Loretta and Gaius bleed red because they and their non-Albinauric [[mounts]] are coded as a single NPC.
* [[Loretta]] was absurdly rumored to be an Albinauric.<ref name="Silver Mirrorshield"/>

==Trivia==
* ''Albinauric'' is a constructed term derived from Latin roots ''[https://en.m.wiktionary.org/wiki/albus albus]'' (pale, white) and ''[[wp:Gold|aurum]]'' (gold), suggesting an approximation of silver made from gold.
* Unlike [[wp:Tadpole|tadpoles]], which gain functional legs as they age into frogs, old Albinaurics eventually lose their disabled legs.0
* Albinaurics were also known as the '''Children of Silver''' in {{1.00}}.<ref name="Blue Silver Mail Armor 1.00"/>
* {{cut}} [[Unused Content|Cut content]]: [[Sorcerer Thops]] specified the Albinaurics and their curses were born of an [[Eternal Cities|Eternal City]] rite, and warns of their grudges against outsiders. He called Young Albinaurics "Frogs."<ref name="Thops">{{cut}} [[Sorcerer Thops]]: ''"Oh, one more thing... Beware the Albinaurics... ...accursed souls born of a forbidden rite of the Eternal City. The curse withered the legs of the old, and silenced the tongues of the Frogs. And now they hold deep grudges for anyone left untouched."''</ref>
* {{cut}} In version 1.00, the [[Academy of Raya Lucaria]] produced [[Albinauric Archer]]s to serve as [[maidens|maids]].<ref name="Blue Silver Mail Armor 1.00">{{cut}} [[Blue Silver Mail Armor]] <small>({{1.00}})</small>: ''"Armor worn by females of the artificial lifeforms known as Children of Silver. Formed of the same silver from which these beings are birthed, this is the only material that doesn't irritate their skin. These maids constructed in Raya Lucaria headed to the Paling Tower, to enter Miquella's service."''</ref>
