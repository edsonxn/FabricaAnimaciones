==Effects==
* Deal 30% (20% in PvP) more Physical Damage and increase maximum [[Stamina]] by 20%, at the cost of decreasing all damage negations by 25%. 
* Lasts for 60 seconds and counts as a Body Buff. 

==Acquisition==
Can be crafted after acquiring the [[Perfumer's Cookbook 2]] from [[The Shaded Castle]]. Head north from the '''Shaded Castle Inner Gate''' Site of Grace and climb up a ladder, then enter the dark room and take the ladder inside to a small rooftop with a body on a chair holding the item. 

==Required items==
An empty [[Perfume Bottle]] is required to craft this item, and will be returned upon its consumption. 
* [[Altus Bloom]] x2
* [[Budding Cave Moss]] x1
* [[Land Octopus Ovary]] x1
* [[Arteria Leaf]] x1

==Notes==
* A very strong damage buff for weapon users, if a bit risky due to the extra damage taken. Be aware that Arteria Leaves are rare to find and somewhat difficult to farm, and also a component for other strong consumables.
