'''{{PAGENAME}}s''' are [[enemies]] and [[bosses]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
Bloodhound Knights are trained as hunters and are known to be unshakable trackers. Without language, they choose their own master whom they will stay loyal to for the rest of their life.<ref>[[Bloodhound Knight Armor]]</ref> 

They wield a [[Bloodhound's Fang]] alongside a [[Bloodhound Claws|Bloodhound Claw]] that allows them to slip past an enemy's guard. They use the [[Ash of War: Bloodhound's Step]] to either move out of harm's way or to close the gap between them and their opponent quickly.

Lesser variants can be found as non-respawning enemies guarding corpses in the overworld. One is found atop a wall near the gate past the [[Isolated Merchant (Academy of Raya Lucaria)|Isolated Merchant at Academy of Raya Lucaria]], another is in one of the rooms past the illusory wall in Volcano Manor, a third is guarding [[Eternal Darkness]] in [[Swamp Lookout Tower]] atop the gate wall near [[Sellia, Town of Sorcery]], and a fourth is guarding the [[Gelmir Knight Set]] inside of [[Gelmir Hero's Grave]] in a room reached by dropping onto rafters below the [[Chariot]]'s path.

==Background==
A Bloodhound Knight was found at the [[Lakeside Crystal Cave]] exit. It appears to be guarding the location of [[Latenna the Albinauric]], forcing her to stay until the location of the [[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right)]] has been revealed.
