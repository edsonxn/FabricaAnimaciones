==Acquisition==
*Dropped by [[Bloodhound Knight Darriwil]] in the [[Forlorn Hound Evergaol]] in Limgrave. [[Blaidd the Half-Wolf]] will direct the [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|Tarnished]] here as part of his questline, and will be available for summoning in the Evergaol before awakening Darriwil after doing so.
