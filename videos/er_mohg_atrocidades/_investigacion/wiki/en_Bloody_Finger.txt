==Effect==
* Activate to [[Invasion|invade]] the world of another player as a [[Bloody Finger (Invader)|Bloody Finger]], with three consumable [[Phantom Bloody Finger]]s to use in that session.
* If the invading player has activated [[Mohg's Great Rune]], they will also receive three [[Phantom Great Rune]]s to use in that session.

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Rose Church]]
* The {{PAGENAME}} is obtained upon returning the bloodsoaked [[Lord of Blood's Favor (Soaked)|Lord of Blood's Favor]] to [[White Mask Varré]] and agreeing to become one of [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Mohg]]'s [[Bloody Fingers]].

==Notes==
* This item can be used as many times as the player wishes.
* A unique background song plays when the Bloody Finger invader engages the [[Host of Fingers]].
* The practical and cosmetic effects for the [[Phantom Bloody Finger]] are the same as the [[Festering Bloody Finger]].
