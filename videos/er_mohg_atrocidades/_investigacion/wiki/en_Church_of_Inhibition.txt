|title=|type=Church|region=[[Liurnia of the Lakes]]|location=|appearances=}}'''Church of Inhibition''' is a location in {{ER}}.
==Overview==
A run-down church  on a hill north-west of the [[Frenzied Flame Village]]. On approach, [[Festering Fingerprint Vyke]] will invade, dropping a [[Fingerprint Grape]] on defeat. 

Within the church is the corpse of a Finger Maiden, sitting on a wooden chair. It can be used to taint the [[Lord of Blood's Favor]] to advance [[Varre|Varré's]] questline.
