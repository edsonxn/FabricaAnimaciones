==Acquisition==
In a treasure chest at [[Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]], guarded by a [[Cleanrot Knight]]. Located north of the Prayer Room grace on a rooftop that you need to jump down to from a rafter. 

==Notes==
*Upgraded with [[Ghost Glovewort 1|Ghost Gloveworts]]. 
*Summons a scythe-wielding [[Cleanrot Knight]]. 
*Deals [[Slash]] and [[Holy]] damage and inflict [[Blood Loss]] build-up with her scythe, [[Pierce]] damage with her off-hand sword, Holy damage with her [[Unique Skill: Miquella's Ring of Light|Miquella's Ring of Light]], and [[Standard]] damage and [[Scarlet Rot]] build-up with her spit attack. 
*Can cast the Cleanrot Knights unique combination of [[Blessing's Boon]] (8 [[HP]]/s) and [[Flame, Protect Me]] (+60% [[Fire]] damage negation) to strengthen both herself and her summoner. 
**This counts as both as a Health Regen Buff and as a [[Buff#Body Buffs|Body Buff]], meaning it will not stack with other buffs of those categories and will override those already present. 
*Fairly durable, but most of her ability lies in her buff, her ability to harass foes from afar, and her ability to help slightly with Blood Loss build-up when attacking with her scythe. 

==Trivia==
*This is one of the Spirit Ashes required for the [[Legendary Ashen Remains (Achievement/Trophy)|Legendary Ashen Remains]] achievement.
