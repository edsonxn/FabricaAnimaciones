The '''Formless Mother''' (姿なき母, ''Sugata-naki Haha''),<ref name="Mohgwyn">[[Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear]]</ref> also known as '''Mother of Truth''' (真実の母, ''Shinjitsu no Haha''),<ref>[[Bloodboon]]</ref> is an [[Outer God]] in {{ER}} and {{SotE}}.

==Overview==
The Formless Mother was encountered by [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Mohg]] deep underground. She is known to crave wounds, and to bestow power upon [[Omen (Lore)|accursed blood]]. Her body can be pierced using [[Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear]], causing the weapon to become coated in [[Bloodflame]], or an arm can be thrust into her body in order to scatter Bloodflame on the ground, using the [[Blood Oath Incantations|incantation]] [[Bloodboon]].

The mother of truth offered the residents of [[Prospect Town]] salvation and transformed them into [[bloodfiends]].<ref>[[Outer God Heirloom]]</ref><ref>[[Bloodfiend Hexer's Ashes]]</ref> [[Sacred Bloody Flesh]] contains blood said to have fallen from the Formless Mother's wound, and is considered a delicacy of the bloodfiends.<ref>[[Sacred Bloody Flesh]]</ref>

==Notes==
* The Formless Mother is omnipresent and can be stabbed from anywhere in the [[Lands Between]] by using a ritual spear.<ref>[[Bloodfiend's Sacred Spear]]</ref>
* Although never directly stated, many details point to the [[Land of Reeds]] being under the influence of the Formless Mother. Descriptions of Reedlanders as having "succumbed to a blood-soaked madness" and their clear favoritism for weapons that cause [[Blood Loss]] all point towards the work of the Outer God. The magical enhancement granded by the forbidden [[Reedlander]] combat technique, [[Ash of War: Seppuku|Seppuku]], may very well be a blessing from the Formless Mother, with the technique acting as a form of offering. Additionally, both [[Okina]] and [[Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger|Eleonora]] join Mohg's [[Bloody Fingers]].

==Trivia==
*The Mother's designation as "Formless" resembles the title of a Great One (conceptually similar to an Outer God) that is indirectly featured in the FromSoftware game ''Bloodborne'', called "[[w:c:bloodborne:Oedon|Formless Oedon]]" (姿なきオドン, ''Sugata-naki Odon''). In fact, both entities are strongly associated with blood.
