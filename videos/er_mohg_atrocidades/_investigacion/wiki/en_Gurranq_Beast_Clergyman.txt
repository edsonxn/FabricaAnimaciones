'''{{PAGENAME}}''', also known as '''[[Beast Clergyman]]''' and '''[[Maliketh, the Black Blade]]''', is an [[NPC]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==

Gurranq is a hulking, bestial high clergyman from [[Crumbling Farum Azula|Farum Azula]] who resides in the [[Bestial Sanctum]] in north-eastern [[Caelid]].<ref>[[Cinquedea]]: ''"Short sword given to high ranking clergymen of Farum Azula."''</ref> Long ago, his terrifying ferocity earned him the name "Death of the Demigods.<ref name="Bestial Constitution">[[Bestial Constitution]]: ''"Incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Fills the body with bestial vigor, alleviating buildup of frost and blood loss."''</ref><ref name="Bestial Sling">[[Bestial Sling]]: ''"Incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Swiftly flings a number of sharp rock shards. This incantation can be cast without delay after performing another action. It is said that in the time before the Erdtree, stones were the first weapons of the beasts who had gained intelligence."''</ref><ref name="Bestial Vitality">[[Bestial Vitality]]: ''"Incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Fills the body with bestial vigor, restoring HP over a period of time. Having gained intelligence, the beasts must have felt how their wildness slipped away as civilization took hold."''</ref><ref name="Stone of Gurranq">[[Stone of Gurranq]]: ''"Superior incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Hurls a boulder before the caster. […] Long ago, Gurranq was a beast of such terrifying ferocity that his former name meant 'Death of the Demigods.'"''</ref><ref name="Beast Claw">[[Beast Claw]]: ''"Incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Creates beast claws that rend the land with shockwaves. […] This incantation represents the fury of Gurranq, his bestial nature returned, as much as it does his restless agitation."''</ref><ref name="Gurranq's Beast Claw">[[Gurranq's Beast Claw]]: ''"Superior incantation taught by Gurranq, the Beast Clergyman. Creates beast claws that rend surroundings with shockwaves."''</ref> Presently, he seeks [[Deathroot]], a product of the [[Rune of Death]] which spread through the [[Lands Between]] following the murder of [[Godwyn the Golden]] on the [[Night of Black Knives]].<ref>[[Deathroot]]</ref> Gurranq consumes Deathroot, although it does not appear to sate him.<ref>[[Gurranq, Beast Clergyman]]: ''"More... I am not Sated. Bring me more Death."''</ref>

Gurranq was assisted in his eradication of the Deathroot by [[D, Hunter of the Dead]], a hunter of [[Those Who Live in Death]].<ref>[[D, Hunter of the Dead]]: ''"If you're inclined to hunt more of Those Who Live in Death, and weed their Deathroot, then I'll introduce you to Gurranq, the beast clergyman. I have a matter of my own to attend to. And the beast himself wishes for someone to take my place."''</ref> However, D passes this duty onto a [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|Tarnished]] after noticing their prowess for weeding Deathroot.<ref>[[D, Hunter of the Dead]]: ''"Ah, a Tarnished, are you? With a prowess for weeding Deathroot."''</ref> The Tarnished brings the Deathroot they find throughout the world to Gurranq, and is given access to bestial incantations in exchange.<ref>[[Gurranq, Beast Clergyman]]: ''"My thanks Tarnished.... Death... My sin... Should not be touched by the hand of man... I shall grant thee... my claws.."''</ref> Once they have fed Gurranq all the Deathroot they can find, Gurranq leaves the sanctum. Although still not sated, he realises that he can do no more, and that his appetite will be his sole companion.<ref>[[Gurranq, Beast Clergyman]]: ''"It is... It is all...consumed. Still, I am not sated... Not nearly sated... Rrarrgh! Marika... Is this...what it is...to sin? Will things...never be the same...again? ... Tarnished...my thanks...for thy long...labour. But I have done...all I can...in this land... Henceforth, mine appetite shall be my sole companion. Farewell."''</ref>

When the Tarnished travels to [[Crumbling Farum Azula]] to claim the Rune of Death, they encounter Gurranq once more. If the Tarnished had given Gurranq all the Deathroots, he is saddened by his friend's betrayal, but nonetheless attacks the Tarnished to protect the Rune of Death from them. Gurranq is overwhelmed, forcing him to use the Rune of Death to empower himself and reveal his true form as [[Maliketh, the Black Blade]],<ref>[[Maliketh, the Black Blade]]: ''"Cower. Before Maliketh, the Black Blade."''</ref> [[Shadowbound Beast|shadowbound beast]] of [[Queen Marika the Eternal]].<ref>[[Remembrance of the Black Blade]]</ref> The Tarnished defeats Maliketh, and unleashes the Rune of Death.

==Questline Progression== 

#''(Optional)'' Speak to D, Hunter of the Dead, after obtaining a Deathroot and he will advise you to visit Gurranq. This can either be done at the start of D's questline in [[Limgrave]], or in [[Roundtable Hold]].
#Travel to the Bestial Sanctum at the north-eastern point of Caelid. The sending gate marked on your map by D if you showed him a Deathroot will lead you straight there. Speak to Gurranq. If you have at least one Deathroot, Gurranq will give you a Clawmark Seal and Beast Eye. The Beast Eye will notify you whenever you are close to another Deathroot.
#Hand over each Deathroot you find to Gurranq in exchange for rewards. After you have given him four Deathroot, reload the area and Gurranq will attack you. After depleting 30% of Gurranq's health, he will be subdued- speak to him and reload the area so that he returns to his original position.
#After giving him the ninth Deathroot, he will give you an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone before disappearing.
#Gurranq will reappear at the end of Crumbling Farum Azula, under the name Beast Clergyman. After reducing his health to 50% he will enter phase 2, under the name Maliketh, the Black Blade. 
#*Maliketh can be fought before completing Gurranq's questline. Doing so will not affect the questline, and Gurranq will remain in the Bestail Sanctum even after Maliketh has been defeated. Likewise, killing Gurranq in the Bestial Sanctum will not affect the fight against Maliketh.
#*Maliketh will have unique dialogue if you complete Gurranq's questline before fighting him in Crumbling Farum Azula.
