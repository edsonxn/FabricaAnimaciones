==Acquisition==
* Lord of Blood's Exultation is obtained by defeating the [[Esgar, Priest of Blood]], in [[Leyndell Catacombs]] beneath [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]].

==Notes==
* Lord of Blood's Exultation increases [[attack power]] by 20% for 20 seconds, when a player or nearby [[enemy]] is inflicted with [[Blood Loss]].
