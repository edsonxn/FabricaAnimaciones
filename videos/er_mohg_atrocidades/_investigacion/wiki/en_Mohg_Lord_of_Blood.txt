'''Mohg, Lord of Blood''' and '''Mohg, the Omen''' are [[Bosses]] {{in|ER}}. '''Luminary Mohg''' is a [[Demigods|Demigod]] and a [[Shardbearers|Shardbearer]] who is encountered in the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]] and at the [[Mohgwyn Palace]].

==Background==
Mohg, also known as the Lord of Blood, was born of the [[Golden Lineage]]—the offspring of [[Queen Marika the Eternal|Queen Marika]] and [[Godfrey, First Elden Lord]]—and was the twin brother of [[Morgott, the Omen King|Morgott]].<ref>[[Mohg's Great Rune]]: ''"Mohg and Morgott are twin brothers, and their Great Runes are naturally similar. But Mohg's rune is soaked in accursed blood, from his devout love for the wretched mire that he was born into far below the earth."''</ref> The twins were accursed [[Omen]] and, as a result, were confined and shackled to the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]] below [[Leyndell, Royal Capital|Leyndell]].<ref>[[Mohg's Shackle]]</ref><!-- However, due to their royal heritage, their horns were left to grow freely.--><ref>[[Regal Omen Bairn]]</ref> 

Unlike his brother, Mohg embraced his Omen [[blood]]. Deep underground, Mohg made contact with an [[Outer God]] known as the [[Formless Mother]], who ignited his accursed blood with [[bloodflame]], granting him powerful magic.<ref>[[Bloodboon]]: ''"Sacred incantation of Mohg, Lord of Blood. Thrust arm into the body of the Formless Mother, then scatter the bloodflame to set the area ablaze. This incantation can be cast while in motion. The mother of truth craves wounds. When Mohg stood before her, deep underground, his accursed blood erupted with fire, and he was besotted with the defilement that he was born into."''</ref><ref>[[Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear]]: ''"Trident of Mohg, Lord of Blood. A sacred spear that will come to symbolize his dynasty. As well as serving as a weapon, it is an instrument of communion with an outer god who bestows power upon accursed blood. The mother of truth desires a wound."''</ref>

Mohg escaped confinement in the wake of the [[Shattering]], claiming a [[Great Rune]] of the [[Elden Ring (concept)|Elden Ring]]. He began establishing his domain from [[Mohgwyn Palace]] and recruiting followers, including his [[Sanguine Noble]]s<ref>[[Sanguine Noble Robe]]: ''"Robe of dyed black cloth featuring gold embroidery. Worn by the nobles who serve the Lord of Blood. The grand metallic pattern on the shoulder is a signifier of the noble rank they intend to claim upon the advent of the new dynasty they are working to install."''</ref> and a murderous [[Tarnished]] known as the [[Bloody Fingers]]. Among these followers was [[Sir Ansbach]], commander of the [[Pureblood Knights]], and [[White Mask Varré|Varré]], a war surgeon who was able to successfully tame Mohg's accursed blood.<ref>[[War Surgeon Gown]]: ''"Bloodstained white gown of the war surgeons who were effectively mercy killers. Of the surgeons that were abducted by the Lord of Blood, none were able to tame the accursed blood. None but Varré, that is; though he was an exception."''</ref>

[[Miquella of the Haligtree|Miquella the Unalloyed]] later embedded himself within his [[Haligtree]] in an attempt to shed his affliction. In this vulnerable and unguarded state, Mohg seized the opportunity to abduct him from the tree.<ref>[[Gideon Ofnir]]: ''"So. The Haligtree, now but a husk... I heard speculation Miquella embedded himself in the Haligtree, but before he could finish, someone cut the tree open and absconded with his infant form."''</ref> Miquella was an [[Empyrean]], thus having the potential of ascending to [[Gods|godhood]]; Mohg intended to raise Miquella to full godhood so that he may rule at his side as consort and take the role of [[Elden Lord]].<ref name=":0">[[Remembrance of the Blood Lord]]: ''"Wishing to raise Miquella to full godhood, Mohg wished to be his consort, taking the role of monarch. But no matter how much of his bloody bedchamber he tried to share, he received no response from the young Empyrean."''</ref> At some point during or after the abduction, Mohg was unknowingly enchanted by Miquella. The circumstances and timeline of this enchantment remain unclear.

After abducting Miquella, Mohg hibernated with him within the [[Cocoon of the Empyrean]], and had his followers render offerings of blood, drenching its chamber in order to facilitate Miquella's awakening.<ref>[[Lord of Blood's Exultation]]: ''"Render up your offerings of blood to your Lord. Drench my consort's chamber. Slake his cocoon's thirst. His awakening shall herald the dawn of our dynasty."''</ref>  But no matter how much of Miquella's bloody bedchamber Mohg tried to share, he would receive no response from the young empyrean.<ref name=":0" />

During this time, Mohg largely escaped the notice of the [[Roundtable Hold]], who were searching for clues as to his whereabouts.<ref>[[Gideon Ofnir]]: ''"There are four more demigods yet to be located. Miquella of the Haligtree, the Unalloyed. His twin, Malenia, the undefeated swordswoman. Lunar Princess Ranni, daughter to Rennala. And the one only known as the Lord of Blood."''</ref>

===Game Events===
Eventually, [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]] came to learn the location of Mohg's palace, where he had hidden alongside the stolen Miquella. When [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]] stumbles upon his chambers, Mohg emerges from a pool of blood, holds Miquella's hand and apologizes for leaving him, ready to confront their "guests". Despite unleashing the full power of his accursed blood, Mohg is unable to overcome the Tarnished, falling at their hands.

Following his death, Mohg's corpse was stolen and transported to the [[Land of Shadow]], where Miquella utilized it for [[Secret Rite Scroll|an ancient ritual]] at the [[Gate of Divinity]]. Mohg's body became a [[vessel]] to house the revived [[Spirits|soul]] of [[Starscourge Radahn|Radahn]], Miquella's [[Promised Consort Radahn|promised consort]].

===Mohg, the Omen===
'''Mohg, the Omen''' is projection encountered in the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]], within the [[Cathedral of the Forsaken]] that lies beyond the {{grace}} [[Forsaken Depths]] Site of Grace. The arena is a fairly small chapel, with pillars on the side that can be used to gain distance. The [[Dung Eater]] is available as a [[Cooperators|summon]] for this fight, provided he has been released from his cell.

Mohg's trident attacks have long delays, and cover a wide range. He uses a number of bloodflame-based attacks, which deal {{dmg|bleed}} [[blood loss]] as well as {{dmg|fire}} [[fire damage]]. [[Mohg's Shackle]] can be used to stun him for a few seconds, allowing for some additional hits.

Defeating '''Mohg, the Omen''' unlocks the {{grace}} [[Cathedral of the Forsaken]] Site of Grace and grants [[Bloodflame Talons]] and {{r}} 100,000 runes. He must be defeated in order to access the [[Frenzied Flame Proscription]].

===Mohg, Lord of Blood===
'''Mohg, Lord of Blood''' is encountered at the top of the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]]. The arena is reasonably wide and open, with some gravestones on each side that act as obstacles. 

This fight plays out much like the '''Mohg, the Omen''' fight, until the second phase, when he performs a [[Unique Skill: Bloodboon Ritual|Bloodboon Ritual]] and subsequently sprouts enormous black wings. During phase 2, many of Mohg's moveset expands and his attacks will cover large areas in fire.

'''Mohg, Lord of Blood''' must be defeated to unlock the ''[[Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree]]'' expansion, and enter the [[Realm of Shadow]].

====Phase 1====
Phase 1 is largely the same as the fight against '''Mohg, the Omen'''. The key difference is the introduction of a countdown. When his health drops to certain levels, he will thrust his spear into the air and chant, causing a red ring to appear around the player. He will do this three times, and once his health reaches 50% he will perform Bloodboon Ritual, dealing huge amounts of damage for each ring and inflicting {{dmg|bleed}} [[blood loss]], while simultaneously recovering his own health. The [[Purifying Crystal Tear]] can be added to the [[Flask of Wondrous Physick]] to significantly decrease the amount of damage dealt by this move.

====Phase 2====
After performing Bloodboon Ritual and recovering much of his health, Mohg will sprout wings and his spear will be imbued with bloodflame. The majority of his attacks will spray bloodflame on the ground, leaving patches of fire that last for a long time. He also now has abilities that utilize his wings to get out of range or to close distance easily.

==Notes==
* Defeating '''Mohg, the Omen''' and '''Mohg, Lord of Blood''' for the first time unlocks the [[Mohg, the Omen (Achievement/Trophy)|"Mohg, the Omen"]] and [[Shardbearer Mohg (Achievement/Trophy)|"Shardbearer Mohg"]] achievements, respectively.
* Mohg's has five fingers on his right hand wielding the trident, but his left hand appears to have six. The finger, the third on his left hand, is actually a stiff, solid claw that sticks out when he summons his Bloodflame Talons.
* Mohg's blood is possibly given to the Bloody Fingers invaders covenant. They have it injected into their finger after completing initiation, and it leads them to develop madness and bloodlust.
* His death pose is visually identical to the "Heart Stolen" death animation the player can suffer during the Promised Consort boss fight; hinting even prior to Shadow of the Erdtree that Mohg was under Miquella's charm all along.
* The specific timing of Mohg assuming the title of '''Lord of Blood''' and the exact sequence of his activities relative to the [[Shattering]] are not well-documented, making the precise timeline of these events unclear.
