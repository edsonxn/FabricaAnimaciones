==Effects==
* Grants a blood blessing to summoned [[Invader]]s, enhancing their attacks with additional [[Blood Loss]] buildup.
* Awards the player a [[Phantom Great Rune]] on successful invasions, which can be used to sacrifice HP to empower nearby enemies.

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]]
* Dropped by [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]] upon defeat at the [[Cocoon of the Empyrean]] in the [[Mohgwyn Palace]].

==Notes==
* Must be restored at the [[Divine Tower of East Altus]], accessible from [[Leyndell]]'s eastern section, by selecting '''Restore the power of the Great Rune'''.
* Shares a [[Divine Towers|Divine Tower]] with [[Morgott's Great Rune]].
