'''Mohg's Shackle''' is a reusable tool in {{ER}}.

==Description==
:''A fetish bathed in golden magic. Shackles were used to bind the accursed people called the Omen, and these ones were made to keep a particular Omen under strictest confinement.''

:''Though faint, the shackles still retain vestiges of power — enough to trap the once-bound Mohg on earth, if only for a short time.''

==Acquisition==
In the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]] beneath [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]]. At the bottom of the large room full of pipes where imps will ambush you.

==Notes==
*This item can not interrupt Mohg's Bloodboon Ritual (the phase 2 transition and when he casts rings around you)
*Does not affect Mohg once he enters phase 2 
*Can be used twice per fight
*Also works against [[Mohg, the Omen]]
