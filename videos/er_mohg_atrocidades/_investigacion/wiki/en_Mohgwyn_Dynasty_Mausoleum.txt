The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[location]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
A large, seemingly ancient temple built into a rocky formation on the west side of [[Mohgwyn Palace]]. The [[Ancient Dynasty|ancient dynasty]] mausoleum is the stronghold of [[Mohg]] and his followers, and is the intended birthplace of the Lord of Blood's new [[Mohgwyn Dynasty]].

The mausoleum can be accessed by traversing the rest of the Mohgwyn Palace area, which is reached via a [[Sending Gate|sending gate]] on the western edge of the [[Consecrated Snowfield]].<!-- This waygate is disabled until a nearby [[Sanguine Noble (Invader)]] is defeated.--> Alternately, using the [[Pureblood Knight's Medal]] granted by [[White Mask Varré]] will teleport the [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|player]] straight to the mausoleum's entrance.

At the top of the structure is a large, open courtyard, in which sits the cocoon of [[Miquella]]. Mohg fiercely guards the [[Empyrean]], and will challenge the player when they approach.
