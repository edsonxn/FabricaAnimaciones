==Acquisition==
* [[Rose Church]] in [[Liurnia of the Lakes]] ([[Fallen Ruins of the Lake]])

After receiving the [[Bloody Finger]] from [[White Mask Varré]] as a reward for [[Lord of Blood's Favor (Soaked)|soaking]] the [[Lord of Blood's Favor]], speak to him again to obtain the Pureblood Knight's Medal.

==Effects==
* Teleports the player to [[Mohgwyn Palace]] near the [[Dynasty Mausoleum Entrance]] [[Sites of Grace|Site of Grace]].
