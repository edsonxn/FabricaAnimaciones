==Effects==
* Consume to gain {{r}} 30,000 [[runes]]
* Tradeable; speak with [[Finger Reader Enia]] and select "'''Receive power from remembrance'''" to gain either [[Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear]] or [[Bloodboon]].

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Cocoon of the Empyrean]] ([[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]])
* The {{PAGENAME}} and [[Mohg's Great Rune]] are obtained upon defeating [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]] at the [[Cocoon of the Empyrean]], located atop the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]] in [[Mohgwyn Palace]].

==Notes==
* Once acquired, this [[Shardbearers|Shardbearer]] Remembrance can be duplicated at any [[Wandering Mausoleum]] with a hanging bell.
* While Remembrances can be duplicated more than once through different duplicators, their rewards may only be unlocked once per [[New Game Plus|playthrough]].
* Max held: 99  
* Max stored: 600  
* Sell value: {{r}} 30,000 [[runes]]
