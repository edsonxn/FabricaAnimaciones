'''Flames''' are a common [[Concept|theme]] in the world of {{ER}}. Not limited to standard [[fire]] attacks, they come in a variety of colors with unique properties.

==List of Flames==
===Black Flame===
[[File:ER Icon Spell Black Flame.png|thumb]]
{{main|Blackflame}}
Black and white-colored flames used by the [[Godskins]] and [[Blackflame Monk]]s, [[Blackflame]] is a weakened variant of [[Destined Death]].<br/>Deals {{dmg|fire}} [[Fire]] damage and gradually drains {{icon|hp|link=HP}} [[HP]] for a short duration.
{{clear}}
===Blasphemous Flame===
[[File:ER Icon Spell Rykard's Rancor.png|thumb]]
Blasphemous flames, also known as '''Taker's Flames''', are the power of [[Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy]] and his [[Blasphemous Blade]].<ref>[[Unique Skill: Taker's Flames]]: ''"Raise the sacred sword aloft to set it ablaze with blasphemous flames, then bring it down to fire off a forward blast. The flames steal HP from those they touch."''</ref><br/>Deals {{dmg|fire}} [[Fire]] damage and steals {{icon|hp|link=HP}} [[HP]].
{{clear}}
===Bloodflame===
[[File:ER Icon Spell Bloodflame Talons.png|thumb]]
{{main|Bloodflame}}
Dark-red flame of the [[Formless Mother]]. Used by [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]], his knights and reluctantly by [[Morgott, the Omen King]].<br/>Deals {{dmg|fire}} [[Fire]] damage and applies a build-up of {{dmg|bleed}} [[Blood Loss]].
{{clear}}
===Destined Death===
[[File:ER Icon Spell Black Blade.png|thumb]]
{{main|Destined Death}}
Black and red-colored flame used by [[Maliketh]] and the [[Black Knife Assassin]]s.<br/>Deals {{dmg|holy}} [[Holy]] damage, reduces maximum {{icon|hp|link=HP}} [[HP]] and gradually drains {{icon|hp|link=HP}} [[HP]] for a short duration.{{clear}}<!--

[[File:ER Icon Spell Ghostflame Breath.png|thumb]]

===Giants' Flame===
[[File:ER Icon Spell Giantsflame Take Thee.png|thumb]]
{{main|Giants' Flame}}
Giants' Flame, also known as the Flame of Ruin or simply the Flame, is a red-orange-colored flame associated with the ancient [[Fell God|Fell God of Fire]]. ===Prince of Death's Flames===
[[File:ER Mausoleum Knight w Sword.png|thumb|<center>Mausoleum Knights carry the Death-Prince's flame</center>]]

The [[Prince of Death's Flame]], also known as Death, is a black-and-gold fire tied to the [[soulless]] body of [[Godwyn the Golden]] that deals {{dmg|holy}} [[Holy damage]] and inflicts {{dmg|death}} [[Death Blight]] buildup. It is wielded by [[Mausoleum Knight]]s and their leader, [[Lhutel the Headless]],<ref name="Lhutel the Headless">[[Lhutel the Headless]]: ''"Spirit of a headless knight who leads the mausoleum soldiers. Wields a lance enrobed in Death and hurls spectral lances at foes. Lhutel sacrificed her life so that in Death she could continue to protect a soulless demigod until their revival, earning her the hero's honor of Erdtree Burial."''</ref> and channeled by the legendary [[Eclipse Shotel]] of [[Castle Sol]].<ref name="Eclipse Shotel">[[Eclipse Shotel]]:''"Storied sword and treasure of Castle Sol that depicts an eclipsed sun drained of color. One of the legendary armaments. In Sol, the sight of an eclipse inspires a dreadful awe, preventing an onlooker from averting his gaze."''</ref><ref name="Death Flare">[[Death Flare]]: ''"Set the lusterless sun ablaze with the Prince of Death's flames, inflicting the death ailment upon foes. Follow up with an additional input to bring down the armament, triggering an explosion."''</ref>

A flame with similar properties burns within [[Fia's Mist]], a [[Death Sorcery]] conceived by [[Fia, Deathbed Companion]]. [[Death-Serving Omen|Prince of Death-Serving Omen]] and [[Death-Serving Commoner|Commoners]] cast the spell at the [[Minor Erdtree (Capital Outskirts)|Minor Erdtree]] in the [[Capital Outskirts]], positioned above the [[Prince of Death's Throne]] in the [[Deeproot Depths]].<ref name="Fia's Mist">[[Fia's Mist]]: ''"Sorcery of Fia, the Deathbed Companion. Creates a deathly mist before the caster, which inflicts Death blight upon those who enter."''</ref><ref name="Fia">[[Fia, Deathbed Companion]]: ''"For I am the companion of Godwyn, Prince of Death."''</ref>
{{clear}}
===Rotten Breath===

Rotten Breath is red-orange flames spewed by [[Putrid Tree Spirit]]s and [[Decaying Ekzykes]].<br/>Applies a build-up of {{dmg|rot}} [[Scarlet Rot]].
