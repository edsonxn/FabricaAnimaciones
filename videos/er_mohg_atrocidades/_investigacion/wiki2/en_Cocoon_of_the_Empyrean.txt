The '''Cocoon of the Empyrean''' is a [[Sites of Grace|Site of Grace]] in {{ER}} and {{SotE}}.

==Overview==
It is found in the boss arena of [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]] atop the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]]. It is revealed after Mohg is slain, and [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]], [[Needle Knight Leda]], and [[Miquella's Followers]] touch Miquella's withered arm to enter the [[Realm of Shadow]].

==Background==
The Cocoon of the Empyrean rests within an enormous pelvic girdle and contains the [[Empyrean]] body of [[Miquella the Kind|Miquella]].
After the [[Battle of Aeonia]] long ago, Miquella placed a powerful charm on the [[Demigods|Demigod]] Mohg.<ref>[[Miquella's Great Rune]]: ''"A Great Rune relinquished by Miquella. Broken and bereft of its bounty, it retains naught but the power to resist charms. Miquella set off for the tower enshrouded by shadow, abandoning everything—his golden flesh, his blinding strength, even his fate. All in an effort to bury the original sin. To embrace the whole of it, and be reborn as a new god."''</ref> cut Miquella from the [[Haligtree Roots|roots]] of the [[Haligtree]] and stole him from the city of [[Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree|Elphael]]. Mohg carried him across the [[Consecrated Snowfield]] and brought him to his [[Mohgwyn Palace]] in the ruins of the [[Ancient Dynasty]]. Mohg has been sharing his accursed blood with Miquella in an attempt to ascend the dormant Empyrean into [[Gods|godhood]] and take his side as the next [[Elden Lord]].<ref >[[Sir Ansbach]]: ''"His Eminence was felled in an honourable duel, and such are the risks of seeking Lordship. Besides, what right have I to complain? I blame the enchantment more than anything."''</ref>

[[Sir Ansbach|Sir Ansbach]], commander of the [[Pureblood Knights]], challenged Miquella in an attempt to dispel the charm cast on Mohg. Although he managed to wound the fearsome Empyrean,<ref>[[Needle Knight Leda]]: ''"He insists that he's nothing but a worn-down, over-the-hill soldier. But in his day, he was the feared commander of the Pureblood Knights, who cleaved open Miquella the Kind with his blood blade."''</ref> he, too, fell under his spell.<ref>[[Sir Ansbach]]: ''"Once, in an attempt to free Lord Mohg from his enchantment, I challenged Tender Miquella, only to have my own heart rather artfully stolen."''</ref><ref>[[Sir Ansbach]]: ''"Kindly Miquella has discarded his Great Rune. The fog that vexed my aging faculties has been lifted."''</ref>
