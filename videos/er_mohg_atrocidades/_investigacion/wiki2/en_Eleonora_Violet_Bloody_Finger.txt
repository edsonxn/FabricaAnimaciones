|location=[[Altus Plateau]]|hp=2512|runes=3116}}'''Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger''' is an [[NPCs|NPC invader]] in ''[[Elden Ring (game)|Elden Ring]]''.

== Overview ==
Hailing from the Land of Reeds<ref name=":0">[[Eleonora's Poleblade]]</ref>, Eleonora is the deadliest [[Bloody Finger (Invader)|Bloody Finger]]<ref name=":1">[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]'s dialogue.</ref> and a partaker of [[Dragon Communion]]<ref>[[Drake Knight Armor]], [[Dragon Communion Seal]]</ref>. She's a proud swordstress who felled many old hands<ref name=":0" />, but now her legacy has been tainted by corrupted blood<ref name=":0" />. Yura has been tracking Eleonora for a long time hoping of cleansing the knight of her cessblood.<ref name=":1" />

==Location==

*[[Altus Plateau]] (Altus Highway Junction)

Invades inside the [[Second Church of Marika]], north-west of the [[Site of Grace]].

==Questline Progression==

#Altus Plateau (Altus Highway Junction)
#*After helping [[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura|Yura]] defeat [[Bloody Finger Ravenmount Assassin]] at the Main Academy Gate of [[Academy of Raya Lucaria|Raya Lucaria]], you'll find him moribund inside the Second Church of Marika; exhausting his dialogues, he'll die leaving behind his [[Nagakiba]] (with the [[Ash of War: Piercing Fang]]). Shortly after, Eleonora will invade you: defeating her will award you with her Poleblade and the [[Purifying Crystal Tear]] that counters [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]]'s phase transitioning curse-attack.

== Notes ==

* If the player enters the church before Yura moves inside, instead of Eleonora, they'll find a [[Sanguine Noble]].
* If [[Morgott, the Omen King]] is defeated before Yura reaches the Second Church of Marika, he'll die leaving behind his Nagakiba at the Seaside Ruins Site of Grace in [[Limgrave]] and [[Shabriri]] will possess his body, moving at the Zamor Ruins Site of Grace in the [[Mountaintops of the Giants]]: at this point, Eleonora will invade regardless, at her usual location.
** If you've also inherited the Frenzied Flame before getting to the Mountaintops, Shabriri won't appear, but the [[Ronin Set]] will be found in his spot.
