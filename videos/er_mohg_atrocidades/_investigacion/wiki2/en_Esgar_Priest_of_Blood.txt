'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an enemy in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
A follower of [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]] who is encountered in the Leyndell Catacombs, hidden within the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]]. Esgar wields a Reduvia and uses blood incantations. He is accompanies by two [[stray]]s that inflict [[Blood Loss]].
