The '''Mohgwyn Dynasty''' is a [[faction]] in {{ER}}.

==Background==
[[File:Mohg carrying Miquella .webp|thumb|Mohg cut Miquella from the Haligtree]]
The Mohgwyn Dynasty was founded by [[Mohg, the Omen]] after he made contact with the [[Formless Mother]], an [[Outer God]] who craves wounds and bestows power upon accursed blood. Mohg would settle his base of operations in the ruins of an [[Ancient Dynasty]], where he would continue to commune with his patron deity along with kidnapping and recruit members to his cause, in an attempt to transform his glorified religious cult into a true dynasty.
