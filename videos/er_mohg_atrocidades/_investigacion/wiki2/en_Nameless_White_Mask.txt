==Overview==
A trio that will invade the player if it explores certain parts of the lake of blood at Mohgwyn's place. 

They used to be war surgeons who were effectively mercy killers. Abducted by the [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Lord of Blood]]<ref>[[War Surgeon Gown]]</ref>, whose curse enlivens the wearers of the [[White Mask|White Masks]] when bloodletting occurs,<ref>[[White Mask]]</ref> none were able to tame the accursed blood.

None but [[White Mask Varré|Varré]], that is; though he was an exception.<ref>[[War Surgeon Gown]]</ref>
