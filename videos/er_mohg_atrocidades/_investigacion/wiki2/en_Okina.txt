'''Bloody Finger Okina''' (血の指、翁) is an NPC cooperator and invader in ''[[Elden Ring (game)|Elden Ring]]''.

==Overview==

Okina (meaning "Old Man" in Japanese) is a great swordsman from the [[Land of Reeds]] whose nickname came from the wooden mask that depicts an old man baring his teeth.<ref> [[Okina Mask]]</ref> He had several disciples in a group called the White Reeds, combatants who seek  proper deaths won honorably through hard-fought combat. The head disciples of Okina were called the [[Inaba, Disciple of Okina|Inaba]] (Japanese name for the leaves of the rice plant).<ref>[[White Reed Armor]]</ref>

As time passed, the more Okina sharpened his mind, the more the absurdities of the world came into stark relief. And so he renounced it all, and rose to a higher plane. There was only himself. His katana. And its mastery.<ref> [[Okina Mask]]</ref> This eventually led him to [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]], and, when Mohg first felt Okina's sword, and madness, upon his flesh, he had a proposal: to offer Okina the life of a demon, whose thirst would never go unsated.<ref> [[Rivers of Blood]]</ref>

Before long, Okina became a demon of a swordsman,<ref> [[Rivers of Blood]]</ref> known as the only to wield an [[Wakizashi|auxiliary weapon]] in his left hand while using a [[Rivers of Blood|full-sized cursed katana]] in his right.<ref> [[Wakizashi]]</ref>

The Inaba traveled to the [[Lands Between]] in pursuit of the master who abandoned them and, desiring nothing more than to die by the sword, also sought proper deaths.<ref>[[White Reed Armor]]</ref> And so, all that was known of them was that their armor and spirit were left lingering in the [[Spiritcaller Cave]].
