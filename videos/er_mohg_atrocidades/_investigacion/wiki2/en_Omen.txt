'''Omen''' (忌み, ''Imi,'' or 忌み子, ''Imigo'') are [[Enemies]] in {{ER}}.

==Background==
Newborn children bearing [[horn]]s were considered [[Gods|divine]] in ancient times<!--, though many perished in infancy-->. A potent [[spirit]]uality suffused their oversized horns, a blessing of the primordial [[Crucible]] believed by the [[hornsent]] to mark them as a chosen people.<ref name="Beast Horn">[[Beast Horn]]: ''"Horn suffused with spirituality. […] The Crucible has a particularly strong influence on the beasts of the realm of shadow, causing many to grow horns despite the characteristics of their species."''</ref><ref name="Horned Bairn">[[Horned Bairn]]: ''"Doll of a tanglehorn bairn. Uses FP to summon vengeful spirits around the caster that autonomously chase down foes. Tangled horns are a symbol of spirituality, but most young born bearing the oversized horns meet a frightfully early demise. These fetishes are made to memorialize them."''</ref><ref name="Immunizing Horn Charm +2">[[Immunizing Horn Charm +2]]</ref> These and other [[aspects of the Crucible|Crucible aspects]] were later deemed [[impurity|impurities]] during the reign of [[Queen Marika]],<ref name="Crucible Feather Talisman">[[Crucible Feather Talisman]]: ''"A talisman fashioned from feathers that embody the aspects of various creatures. Said to have grown on the human body long ago. […] A vestige of the crucible of primordial life. Born partially of devolution, it was considered a signifier of the divine in ancient times, but is now increasingly disdained as an impurity as civilization has advanced."''</ref> who excluded those who bore them from her [[Grace of Gold]].{{cite|}}

The Omen's horns are imbued with [[wraiths]] and their blood is tainted.<ref name="Remembrance of the Omen King">[[Remembrance of the Omen King]]: ''"Though born one of the graceless Omen, Morgott took it upon himself to become the Erdtree's protector. He loved not in return, for he was never loved. But nevertheless love it he did."''</ref><ref name="Bloodboon">[[Bloodboon]]: ''"The mother of truth craves wounds. When Mohg stood before her, deep underground, his accursed blood erupted with fire, and he was besotted with the defilement that he was born into."''</ref><ref>[[Stranded Souls]]: ''"Mohg, you rotten Omen. Your blood is cursed."''</ref><ref name="Morgott's Cursed Sword">[[Morgott's Cursed Sword]]: ''"Warped blade of shifting hue used by Morgott, the Omen King. The accursed blood that Morgott recanted and sealed away reformed into this blade."''</ref> Without the Grace of Gold, they were shunned from society, their souls barred from returning to the [[Erdtree]].<ref name="Seedbed Curse">[[Seedbed Curse]]: ''"Curse grown on a corpse killed and defiled by the Dung Eater. A tender pox afflicted with omen horns. The Dung Eater cultivates the seedbed curse on corpses. By doing so he prevents dead souls returning to the Erdtree, leaving them forever cursed. One of the most loathsome things found in all the Lands Between."''</ref> Royal Omen, including the twins born to Queen Marika and [[Lord Godfrey]], were secretly banished to the [[Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]] below [[Leyndell, Royal Capital|Leyndell]], buried and shackled by [[gold]]en magic for eternity.<ref name="Regal Omen Bairn">[[Regal Omen Bairn]]: ''"Omen babies born of royalty do not have their horns excised, but instead are kept underground, unbeknownst to anyone, imprisoned for eternity. These memorial fetishes are fashioned in secret."''</ref><ref>[[Margit's Shackle]]: ''"A fetish bathed in golden magic. Shackles were used to bind the accursed people called the Omen, and these ones were made to keep a particular Omen under strictest confinement. Though faint, the shackles still retain vestiges of power — enough to trap the once-bound Margit on earth, if only for a short time."''</ref> In contrast, the horns of commoners were severed,<ref name="Omen Bairn">[[Omen Bairn]]: ''"Doll of a curseborn bairn. Uses FP to unleash wraiths that chase down foes. Omen babies have all their horns excised, causing most to perish. These fetishes are made to memorialize them. 'Please, don't hate me, or curse me. Please.'"''</ref> a brutal surgical procedure that few survived.

During times of war, conscripted Omen received curved cleavers that took advantage of their brute strength, though a worsening curse placed upon the blades ensured they would later be reclaimed.<ref name="Omen Cleaver">[[Omen Cleaver]]: ''"Heavy-bladed curved sword of colossal size awarded to Omen as a tool of war. This weapon is made to take advantage of brute strength. The pattern etched upon the blade is the remnant of a deteriorative malediction. Indeed, when bestowing a weapon, preparations must be made for taking it away."''</ref> Additionally, wicked, horned elder spirits tormented Omen in their nightmares,<!-- This should be higher up because it is not linked to the weapon --> and masked [[perfumers]] imitated these hideous spirits.<ref>[[Omensmirk Mask]]: ''"Mask with long, hideously twisted horns worn by the Omenkillers. Increases strength. Bears the smirking face of an elder, twisted in wicked delight. This visage is carved in the image of the evil spirits that haunt the Omen in their nightmares."''</ref> [[Omenkiller]]s mercilessly hunted and subdued Omen with blades decorated with amputated Omen horns.<ref>[[Great Omenkiller Cleaver]]: ''"The blade of this huge, loathsome cleaver comprises a row of amputated Omen horns. Weapon of slaughter wielded by Omenkillers. The hideous horns cause blood loss, adding vibrant colors to the ongoing mayhem."''</ref> However, [[Perfumer Tricia]] and her partner instead provided aromatic treatments to the help Omen as they died.<ref name=":0">[[Traveler's Hat]]: ''"Hat of a traveling perfumer of no renown. An associate of a healer, he is said to be in search of new aromatics and flower gardens, in hopes of treating Misbegotten, Omen, and all those seen as impure."''</ref><ref name="Perfumer Tricia">[[Perfumer Tricia]]: ''"Tricia was once known as a healer who dedicated her efforts to treating Misbegotten, Omen, and all those seen as impure. When her efforts failed, she was their companion as they died, watching over them to ensure that they could pass peacefully, free of pain. A tale akin to the origins of the deathbed companions."''</ref>

==Characteristics==
Omen are hulking, blue-skinned brutes with small heads and silver hair. They have four [[fingers]] and toes, and twisted [[horns]] sprouting from their large bodies, though lowborn Omen bear bleeding stumps instead. [[Demigods|Demigod]] Omen, such as [[Morgott, the Omen King]] and [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]], have scales, tails, and even black-feathered wings.

Accursed Omen, born of royalty, possess some horn growth but are forced to live underground in secrecy.<ref name="Regal Omen Bairn"/> They harbor some amount of wraiths, which they can expel from their mouths at foes. The Strong Accursed Omen, capable of unleashing a barrage of wraiths in a fiery explosion, consequently imbuing their weapons with their power. In this empowered state, they can slam their weapons with explosive force to release more wraiths.

Omen wrapped in black cloaks aligned with [[Death-Serving Commoner]]s in worship of the [[Prince of Death]], who was once [[Godwyn the Golden]].

===Omen Cleaver===

The Omen wields an [[Omen Cleaver]], a heavy-bladed, curved sword etched with a pattern that is the remnant of a deteriorating malediction.<ref name="Omen Cleaver"/> The swords have a deceptively long reach, which the Omen take full advantage of with their fighting style.

A hooded Omen with an Omen Cleaver can be found praying in front of a [[Minor Erdtree]] in [[Altus Plateau]] alongside human [[Death-Serving Commoner|Death-prince Followers]]. Another can also be found in Altus, hidden in the depths of [[Auriza Hero's Grave]]. Though their horns may be excised, they can summon Fia's Mist.

==Notes==
* The [[Fell Twins]] duo boss encountered on the bridge leading to the [[Divine Tower of East Altus]] are both Omen. Other Omen bosses include [[Margit, the Fell Omen]], [[Mohg, the Omen]], [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]], and [[Morgott, the Omen King]].
* The [[Dung Eater]] has the heart of an Omen and wears red armor resembling an Omen with severed horns. He spreads the [[Seedbed Curse]] that produces more Omen,<ref name="Seedbed Curse"/> and his [[Mending Rune of the Fell Curse]] turns everyone into Omen.<ref name="Omen Helm">[[Omen Helm]]: ''"Malformed helm resembling an Omen with its horns cut off. Worn by the Dung Eater, its form is a vision of the landscape of his mind, and of his appearance as he wished to see it. The heart of an Omen without the body to match; could there be any crueler existence? What does it matter, then, if the curse claims it all."''</ref><ref name="Mending Rune of the Fell Curse">[[Mending Rune of the Fell Curse]]: ''"Loathsome rune gestated by the Dung Eater. Used to restore the fractured Elden Ring when brandished by the Elden Lord. The reviled curse will last eternally, and the world's children, grandchildren, and every generation hence, will be its pustules. If Order is defiled entirely, defilement is defilement no more, and for every curse, a cursed blessing."''</ref>
* [[Perfumer Tricia]] and her associates<ref name=":0" /> dedicated their efforts to treating Omen and other impure beings. When her efforts failed, she instead became their companion when they died, ensuring they could pass peacefully.<ref name="Perfumer Tricia"/>

==Trivia==
* Internal names: '''ShunnedChildSword''' (ID: 214000)  and '''ShunnedChildAxa''' (ID: 214010)
* Localization Notes
** The Omen are called 忌み (''Imi''), which would mean "Abomination" in this context.<ref>[https://jisho.org/search/%E5%BF%8C%E3%81%BF Jisho]</ref>
**They are also occasionally referred to as 忌み子 (''Imigo''), which means "Abominable Child", "Unwanted Child", or "Shunned Child."<ref>[https://jisho.org/search/%E5%BF%8C%E3%81%BF%E5%AD%90 Jisho]</ref>
**Margit's "Fell Omen" title is 忌み鬼 (''Imi Oni''), meaning "Abominable Demon."<ref>[https://jisho.org/search/%E5%BF%8C%E3%81%BF%E9%AC%BC Jisho]</ref>
** The Mending Rune of the Fell Curse is called 忌み呪いの修復ルーン (''Imi Noroi no Shūfuku Rūn'') "Mending Rune of the Abomination Curse" or "Abominable Curse", where 忌み (Imi) is the same word localised as "Omen." Therefore, "Mending Rune of the Omen Curse" would be a localization in line with the rest of the text.
**The Omen Bairn items are called 水子 (''Mizuko''), which can refer to a newborn baby, or an aborted, stillborn, or miscarried fetus.
***水子供養 (''Mizuko kuyō'') is a Japanese Buddhist memorial service for those who have had a miscarriage, stillbirth, or abortion. The ceremony is meant to comfort parental grief, guilt, or the soul of the fetus, sometimes in fear of its vengeful spirit. Statues of Jizō, a Bodhisattva thought to protect children, are commonly offered by temples, which may be the inspiration for the Omen Bairns.<ref>[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizuko_kuy%C5%8D Wikipedia Article]</ref> 
* Possibly inspired by the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shope_papilloma_virus Shope papilloma virus] that causes rabbits to form horn-like growths.
