'''Purifying Crystal Tear''' is a [[Crystal Tear]] in {{ER}}.

==Description==
{{Description

==Use==
This item makes the fight against [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]] significantly easier by mitigating the boss's curse-effect, significantly reducing damage taken from his Bloodboon Ritual. This effect works even if used before the first step of the ritual countdown (Tres).

It does not prevent Mohg from recovering HP during the attack, however, and has no use outside of his fight.

==Acquisition==
Defeat [[Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger]] when advancing [[Yura|Yura's]] questline.
