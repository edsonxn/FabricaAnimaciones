==Acquisition==
Trade the [[Remembrance of the Omen King]], gained by defeating [[Morgott, the Omen King]], and 5000 [[Runes]] with [[Finger Reader Enia]].

Alternatively, use one of the [[Wandering Mausoleums]] with a bell to duplicate one (can only obtain one per playthrough using either method).
==Effect==
Using this item will make the player-character brace themselves for a second before unleashing a close-ranged explosion that'll launch human-sized (or smaller) opponents away from them before sending out 10 long-ranged [[Wraith|wraith projectiles]] that'll track any target that the user was locked on to as they were ejected from their body. These wraiths travel in three groups, the first two groups being comprised of 3 projectiles each and the final having 4, each group also have their own defined travel speed, leading to three distinct rolling windows whenever the player is fighting against this tool in [[PvP]].

As it deals [[Holy|Holy Damage]]; any holy-boosting equipment or spell will further increase the damage from this item.
Each piece of the [[Omen Set]] will also increase the damage of this item by 5%, meaning wearing the full set will increase it by 20%.
