==Effects==
* {{dmg|bleed}} [[Blood Loss]] in the vicinity raises [[Attack Power]] by 10% for 20 seconds (6% in [[PvP]]).

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Mohgwyn Palace]]
* White Mask is dropped by one of the three [[Nameless White Mask]] invaders in [[Mohgwyn Palace]], northwest of the {{grace}} [[Palace Approach Ledge-Road]] [[Site of Grace]], in the blood-filled lake. The invader that drops it is found adjacent to the [[Giant Crow]] near the lake exit from the tunnel.
