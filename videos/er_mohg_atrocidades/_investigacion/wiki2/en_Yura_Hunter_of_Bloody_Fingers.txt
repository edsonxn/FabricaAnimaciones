'''Bloody Finger Hunter Yura''' is an [[NPC]] [[character]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
A ronin warrior in an iron kasa reminiscent of the straw hats worn in the [[Land of Reeds]],<ref name="Iron Kasa">[[Iron Kasa]]: ''"Iron kasa of the man who calls himself Shabriri. Crafted to imitate the woven straw hats worn in the Land of Reeds."''</ref> Yura was a [[hunter]] of [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Mohg]]'s [[Bloody Fingers]].<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"I am Yura, as you might recall, hunter of Bloody Fingers."''</ref> He understood the hidden dangers of [[Dragon Communion]] and was once close to [[Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger]], a former [[Drake Knight]] for whom he harbored deep feelings.<ref name="Ronin's Armor">[[Ronin's Armor]]: ''"Armor of the man who calls himself Shabriri. Highly functional and heavily worn. The man, once known as Yura, kept a woman in the deepest reaches of his heart."''</ref><ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"I'm dying to see you, Eleonora... Violet Bloody Finger..."''</ref> He wielded the unblockable [[Ash of War: Piercing Fang|Piercing Fang]] technique with deadly precision.<ref name="Piercing Fang">[[Ash of War: Piercing Fang]]: ''"Skill used by Yura, the Bloody Finger Hunter. Starting with the blade held horizontally, make a powerful thrust that cannot be blocked."''</ref>

===Game events===
Yura can first be found resting beneath a [[Fallen Ruins|fallen ruin]] south of the [[Dragon-Burnt Ruins]] in [[Limgrave]]. He warns [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]] to stay away from [[Agheel Lake]], where [[Flying Dragon Agheel]] roosts.<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Beware. A great dragon roosts at Agheel Lake. And it's as fearsome as it is majestic. So, unless you're mad, or wish to be burned alive... stay clear of the lake."''</ref> However, he offers his aid if the player is brave enough to hunt the mighty [[dragon]]. If the Tarnished slays Agheel, Yura speaks of the island [[Church of Dragon Communion]],<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"That heart you brought back. It's used in Dragon Communion. If you should find yourself overcome by hunger for the heart, yearning for its strength, then seek the decrepit church on the little island off the western coast."''</ref> and cautions against Dragon Communion's corrupting influence.<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Those who partake in Dragon Communion will one day shed their humanity."''</ref> 

When the Tarnished is ambushed by [[Bloody Finger Nerijus]] on the [[Murkwater River]], Yura intervenes to thwart the [[invasion]].<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"If it isn't Nerijus. The Bloody Finger. The end is nigh. For you, and your cessblood."''</ref> Later, Yura travels to the [[Academy of Raya Lucaria]], where the Tarnished may repay his aid by assisting him against a [[Bloody Finger Ravenmount Assassin|Ravenmount Assassin]].<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Ah, oh, it's you. Thanks for your help there. That Bloody Finger was a thorn in my side. And now I'm finally rid of him."''</ref> There, Yura urges them to avoid Eleonora, the deadliest of all Bloody Fingers.<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Yes, I've been tracking Eleonora for quite some time. She is the deadliest of all Bloody Fingers. She's felled many an old hand already."''</ref>

Yura ultimately tracks Eleonora to the [[Second Church of Marika]] on the [[Altus Plateau]]. In their final battle, he suffers a mortal wound to sever the [[Bloody Finger]] from her hand.<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Eleonora, it seems I am no match for you. But I've learned a thing or two myself, you see. I've sliced the finger off."''</ref> As the Tarnished arrives too late, Yura implores Eleonora to renounce the [[cessblood]] before dying in their presence.<ref>[[Bloody Finger Hunter Yura]]: ''"Please, please, Eleonora, yield to the cessblood no longer."''</ref>

After his death, Yura's body becomes host to [[Shabriri]], who claims that Yura willingly surrendered his vessel.<ref name="Ronin's Armor" /><ref>[[Shabriri]]: ''"I fear that you were previously acquainted with this vessel. Well, that is most unfortunate. For he is dead. As for his flesh, he gave it to me, Shabriri."''</ref>

===Questline Progression===

#''(Optional)'' [[Limgrave]]
#*The following two steps can be completed in either order. His dialogue will vary depending on where he is met first.
#*Yura can be found under a [[Fallen Ruins|fallen Azula ruin]] near the {{grace}} [[Seaside Ruins]] [[site of grace]], southwest of [[Agheel Lake]]. Exhaust his dialogue and his [[summon sign]] will appear in Agheel Lake to assist against [[Flying Dragon Agheel]]. Speak to him again after defeating Agheel for further dialogue.
#*Enter the [[Murkwater River]], the ravine north of Agheel Lake, and head towards [[Murkwater Cave]]. [[Bloody Finger Nerijus]] will [[Invasion|invade]] you. After fighting Nerijus for a while, Yura appears to assist you. After defeating Nerijus, continue along the ravine to find Yura under a bridge. Exhaust his dialogue. 
#[[Liurnia of the Lakes]]
#*A [[Adversaries|red summon sign]] can be found northeast of the {{grace}} [[Raya Lucaria Main Gate]]. Interact with it to assist Yura in defeating [[Bloody Finger Ravenmount Assassin]]. After defeating the foe, Yura can be found on the bridge near where the summon sign was. Speak to him to receive a [[Smithing Stone 5|Smithing Stone <nowiki>[5]</nowiki>]].
#[[Altus Plateau]]
#*Travel to the [[Second Church of Marika]], located northwest of the {{grace}} [[Altus Highway Junction]] site of grace, where Yura lies on the ground. Speak to him, and then pick up the [[Nagakiba]]. Shortly after, [[Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger]] will invade. Defeat her to receive [[Eleonora's Poleblade]] and the [[Purifying Crystal Tear]].
#[[Mountaintops of the Giants]]
#*Once you travel to the [[Mountaintops of the Giants]], Yura's quest will be locked regardless of which steps have been completed. [[Shabriri]] will be waiting near the {{grace}} [[Zamor Ruins]] site of grace, in possession of Yura's reanimated body. The Nagakiba can be found on the ground in whichever location he would have been at, depending on how far into his questline you have progressed.
