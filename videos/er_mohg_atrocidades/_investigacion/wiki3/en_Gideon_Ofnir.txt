{{Nihongo|'''Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing'''|百智卿、ギデオン オーフニール|Hyakuchikyō, Gideon Ōfunīru}} is an [[NPC]] and [[boss]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
[[File:Eldenringgideonofnir.jpg|thumb|Sir Gideon Ofnir laid to rest]]
Gideon Ofnir is the [[Tarnished]] leader of [[Roundtable Hold]] and the adoptive father of [[Nepheli Loux]].<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"I understand you've been speaking to Nepheli. She is my daughter. I took her in when she lost the guidance of grace."''</ref> Called to the Lands Between by the guidance of Grace, Gideon wishes to become Elden Lord.<ref>[[Nepheli Loux, Warrior]]: ''"I would have done anything for him, to place him on the throne of Elden Lord."''</ref> To this end he accumulates information on the [[Shardbearers]], employing a network of spies<ref>[[Eye Surcoat]]</ref> to aid in his hunt for knowledge. Initially, he is apprehensive of [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]], but after they have claimed a [[Great Rune]] he becomes willing to share his knowledge of the remaining shardbearers.

In the past, Gideon took in Nepheli Loux after she lost sight of the guidance of Grace, taking advantage of her youthful credulity.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"Tough a mere axe-wielding barbarian, her youthful credulity suited my purposes. So I put her to work."''</ref>  He promised her that he would not allow the downtrodden to be cheated any longer if he became Elden Lord.<ref>[[Nepheli Loux, Warrior]]: ''"He once told me that if he became Elden Lord, he would never allow the downtrodden to be cheated ever again."''</ref> He also became acquainted with [[Seluvis|Preceptor Seluvis]] and [[Dolores the Sleeping Arrow Puppet|Dolores the Sleeping Arrow]], although he and Seluvis would go their separate ways because of Dolores.<ref>[[Dolores the Sleeping Arrow Puppet]]</ref> 

At some point, Gideon engaged in a long exchange with the [[Two Fingers]], and in doing so learned that everything—including the Erdtree and the Two Fingers themselves—had been broken.<ref>[[Lord's Divine Fortification]]</ref> He also glimpsed into the will of [[Queen Marika the Eternal]], shuddering in fear at an end that should not be.<ref>[[All-Knowing Armor]]</ref> He concluded from this that the queen wished for the Tarnished to struggle eternally.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"Queen Marika has high hopes for us. That we continue to struggle. Unto eternity."''</ref>

When a [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|Tarnished champion]] makes their way to the [[Roundtable Hold]], the first in a long time, Gideon is initially dismissive of them, welcoming them to the Hold but calling them a mere visitor. Gideon encourages the Tarnished to seek a [[Great Runes|Great Rune]] in order to gain audience with the [[Two Fingers]] and be welcomed as a proper member of the Roundtable Hold. 

True to his word, Gideon inducts them as a true member of the Roundtable upon their meeting with the Two Fingers, and shares with them some of the information he has amassed on the [[Shardbearers]]. 

Although Gideon had amassed knowledge on many of the shardbearers, he had not ascertained the whereabouts of four demigods: [[Ranni the Witch]]; [[Mohg, Lord of Blood]]; [[Malenia, Blade of Miquella]]; and [[Miquella of the Haligtree]]. In his search for the latter, he sends his men to ransack the [[Village of the Albinaurics]] in [[Liurnia of the Lakes]], knowing that the Albinaurics possess half of a [[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right)|secret medallion]] that would open the path to the Haligtree. The slaughter of the Albinaurics causes Nepheli to lose faith in him, which causes him to abandon her.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"Indeed, it seemed the whelp harboured suspicions. So I had no further use for her."''</ref> He also  sought [[Latenna]], an Albinauric woman with knowledge of the location of the other half of the medallion.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"Find the Albinauric woman. She hides in a cave to the west of the Laskyar Ruins which jut from the mist-shrouded lake of Liurnia, together with her great wolf. She knows the location of the medallion's counterpart, I am sure."''</ref> If the Tarnished claims one half of the medallion, Gideon's underling [[Ensha of the Royal Remains|Ensha]] will ambush them in Roundtable Hold, apparently of his own volition.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"Oh, my apologies for that nasty business. Ensha got rather ahead of himself, it seems."''</ref> After Ensha is defeated, Gideon compensates the Tarnished by sharing the possible whereabouts of the medallion's other half.

When the [[Erdtree]] rejects the [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|Tarnished]], forcing them to contemplate journeying to the [[Forge of the Giants]] to burn the thorns barring entry into the Erdtree, Gideon encourages them to take no heed of the Fingers' warning that this would be a cardinal sin. He also offers to reward the Tarnished for any information they can deliver on the four great runes whose locations are unknown. If the Tarnished pursues this quest to its ending, finding the cocoon of [[Miquella of the Haligtree|Miquella]] within the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum]], Gideon thanks them, declaring that this is one of the last pieces he needs to put everything together.

After the Tarnished burns the Erdtree and unseals the [[Rune of Death]], Gideon makes his way to [[Leyndell, Ashen Capital]], where he confronts the Tarnished in the Erdtree Sanctuary. Upon his defeat, he warns them that a Tarnished could not become a Lord, and that a man cannot kill a god.<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"I know...in my bones... A Tarnished cannot become a Lord. Not even you. A man cannot kill a god..."''</ref>

==Character Information==
Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing is a boss encounter in Leyndell, Ashen Capital. He utilises both sorceries and incantations in combat, and learns new spells depending on whether you have defeated [[Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy|Rykard]], Malenia, or Mohg. He only has seven memory slots, and knows six spells by default, meaning that the spells learned by defeating the aforementioned bosses can replace his default spells. If two of these bosses have been defeated, Gideon will lose Comet Azur. If all three have been defeated, he will lose Triple Rings of Light.

===Questline Progression===

#[[Roundtable Hold]]
#* ''(Optional)'' If you visit Roundtable Hold before defeating a shardbearer, Gideon can be found in the central chamber of Roundtable Hold. After exhausting his dialogue he will move to his study.
#[[Roundtable Hold]]
#*After defeating a shardbearer and speaking to [[Finger Reader Enia]], Gideon will welcome you as a member of Roundtable Hold, and will offer you information on [[Godrick the Grafted]], [[Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon]], [[Starscourge Radahn]], [[Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy]], and [[Morgott, the Omen King]]. He can also discuss Nepheli Loux if you have spoken to her. 
#[[Roundtable Hold]]
#*''(Optional)'' If you obtain either half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion, Ensha will ambush you the next time you return to Roundtable Hold. After defeating him, speak to Gideon to learn the location of the other half. 
#*''(Optional)'' If you have obtained Seluvis's Potion, you may give it to Gideon to dispose of it. This will remove it from your inventory, preventing you from obtaining [[Nepheli Loux Puppet]] or [[Dung Eater Puppet]]. 
#[[Roundtable Hold]]
#*Resting at any Site of Grace in [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]] will cause Gideon to receive new dialogue about the unlocated Demigods.
#[[Roundtable Hold]]
#*Gideon will now offer rewards in exchange for meeting certain requirements:
#**Black Flame's Protection: activate a Site of Grace in Miquella's Haligtree.
#**Lord's Divine Fortification: defeat Malenia, Blade of Miquella.
#**Fevor's Cookbook 3: activate a Site of Grace in Mohgwyn Palace.
#**Law of Causality: defeat Mohg, Lord of Blood.
#[[Leyndell, Ashen Capital]]
#*Gideon will be present in the Erdtree Sanctuary as a boss encounter after you have defeated [[Maliketh, the Black Blade]]. Any previous parts of his questline will now be locked, however the rewards from the previous step can be purchased from the [[Twin Maiden Husks]].

*The [[Omenkiller]] in the [[Village of the Albinaurics]] is implied by [[Nepheli]] to be one of Gideon's agents. Due to the connection between Omenkillers and [[perfumer]]s, the [[Depraved Perfumer]] in the village may be working with the Omenkiller.
