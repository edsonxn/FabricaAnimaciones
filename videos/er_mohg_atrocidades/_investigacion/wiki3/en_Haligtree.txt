The '''Haligtree''' (聖樹, ''Seiju'') is a [[Trees|great tree]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
The Haligtree is a massive, withering tree covered in fungi, extending from a stump in the northern [[Lands Between]]. It lies far north of [[Leyndell, Royal Capital]], and off the coast of the [[Consecrated Snowfield]].<ref name="greatshield">[[Haligtree Crest Greatshield]]</ref> Pilgrims must present a [[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right)|secret medallion]] at the [[Grand Lift of Rold]] to access the Consecrated Snowfield.<ref>[[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left)]]</ref> From there, they follow a trail of lights,<ref>[[Stranded Souls|Stranded Soul]]: ''"Show me the path, O gentle lights. The promised path to the Haligtree!"''</ref><ref name="elphael">[[Stranded Souls|Stranded Soul]]: ''"... Only...a little further now... Show me the path, O gentle lights...the path to Elphael."''</ref> cross the river to [[Ordina, Liturgical Town]],<ref>[[Latenna the Albinauric]]: ''"We have reached the land of Miquella's Haligtree. These great snow-laden lands stretch far to the north. And beyond the ancient bowers, and the liturgical town of Ordina, lies the place to which I must return."''</ref> and use a [[Sending Gate]] to reach the Haligtree.<ref>[[Map Consecrated Snowfield|Map: Consecrated Snowfield]]</ref>

The Haligtree is divided into two areas: [[Miquella's Haligtree]] and [[Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]]. The upper area, [[Miquella's Haligtree]], is a sparse canopy populated by [[Giant Ant]]s and [[Oracle Envoy]]s. This section contains [[Haligtree Town]], an open and organic treehouse refuge for the unwanted that features [[statues]] of the twin [[Demigods]]. The lower section, [[Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]], is a robust stone fortress occupied by [[Haligtree Knight]]s and [[Cleanrot Knight]]s who serve [[Miquella the Kind|Miquella]] and [[Malenia, Blade of Miquella|Malenia]]. Both areas are made from different materials and for different purposes, though Haligtree Town appears far newer and cleaner than the brace. Its design features more complex curved surfaces, and it has more of the intricate knotwork patterns associated with Miquella's Haligtree.

==Background==
Long ago, the [[Empyreans|Empyrean]] Miquella abandoned [[Golden Order|Golden Order Fundamentalism]] upon realizing it could not cure his twin sister Malenia's [[Scarlet Rot|scarlet rot]].<ref>[[Radagon's Rings of Light]]</ref> Seeking to create a new order of [[Unalloyed Gold]], he settled on the Haligtree as his sanctuary.<ref name="greatshield"/> 

After the [[Battle of Aeonia]],<ref>[[Redmane Freyja]]: ''"Long ago, I was stricken by scarlet rot in the Swamp of Aeonia. Immobile, feverish, and in great pain, I was entirely resigned to death. I was left behind, and only Kindly Miquella was good enough to seek me out. My wound was swollen and festering—exuding a most pungent odour<!--sic-->—and yet he drained the poison from it."''</ref> Miquella watered the sapling with his own Empyrean blood.<ref>[[Haligtree Knight Armor]]</ref> He later embedded himself within a cocoon in the tree's roots to nurture it, attempting to grow it into an [[Erdtree]].<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"I heard speculation Miquella embedded himself in the Haligtree, but before he could finish, someone cut the tree open and absconded with his infant form."''</ref> However, his efforts were interrupted when [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Mohg, the Lord of Blood]], abducted him from the Haligtree. Under Miquella's charm,<ref>[[Bewitching Branch]]</ref><ref>[[Sir Ansbach]]: ''"Once, in an attempt to free Lord Mohg from his enchantment, I challenged Tender Miquella, only to have my own heart rather artfully stolen."''</ref> Mohg zealously sought to elevate the Empyrean to [[Gods|godhood]] and establish the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty]] as Miquella's consort.<ref>[[Remembrance of the Blood Lord]]</ref> Without Miquella, the Haligtree began to wither.

Afterward, [[Cleanrot Knight Finlay]] carried Malenia's slumbering body to the Haligtree, placing her at its roots. There, Malenia awaited her brother's return while slowly corrupting the tree with the scarlet rot.<ref>[[Malenia's Winged Helm]]</ref>

Nevertheless, the Haligtree became a refuge for societal outcasts, including the [[Misbegotten]] and [[Kindred of Rot]].<ref>[[Rotten Winged Sword Insignia]]</ref> [[Loretta]], a former [[Carian Knight|Knight of Caria]],<ref>[[Loretta's War Sickle]]</ref><ref>[[Miquellan Knight's Sword]]</ref> established a sanctuary for the [[Albinaurics]] within its boughs,<ref>[[Loretta's Mastery]]</ref><ref>[[Royal Knight Helm]]</ref> turning the area into a sacred haven for their kind.<ref>[[Ivory Sickle]]</ref><ref>[[Old Albus]]: ''"A chosen land awaits us Albinaurics. The medallion is the key that leads to the city. It's only a quaint treasure, for we who cannot make the journey. But for dear Latenna, it is needed. To fulfil<!--sic--> her purpose."''</ref>

==Notes==
* The form of a young person with a hole in their torso can still be seen in the roots of the Haligtree where Miquella's cocoon once rested within it.
* The current Haligtree appears to have grown from the stump of the older tree originating from Miquella's cocoon.
* Haligtree game area has two sets of roots that often appear alongside one another. The larger, wider, and darker ones are older than Miquella's.
<!--* [[Sage Gowry]] sent [[Millicent's Sisters]] to the Haligtree.-->
* The master of Castle Sol holding half of the Medallion required to access the Haligtree would have been allowed to enter it depending on the success of the [[Eclipse]] in reviving a soulless Demigod.<ref>[[Stranded Souls|Stranded Soul]] ([[Castle Sol]]): ''"Lord Miquella, forgive me. The sun has not been swallowed. Our prayers were lacking. Your comrade remains soulless... I will never set my eyes upon it now... Your divine Haligtree..."''</ref> 
* [[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing|Gideon Ofnir]] sought the path to the Haligtree, sending his shadow, [[Ensha of the Royal Remains|Ensha]], to retrieve the secret medallion from the [[Village of the Albinaurics]]. In the process, Ensha killed [[Latenna the Albinauric|Lattena]]'s wolf companion, [[Lobo]], but ultimately failed in his mission.<ref>[[Latenna the Albinauric]]: ''"I told the all-hearing brute already. That I possess no such medallion. Or have you come to take more from me? Was my other half not enough? I told the bastard in bone armour already."''</ref>
* The Haligtree Secret Medallion depicts it with green or [[viridian]] [[amber]].

==Trivia==
* Haligtree is a combination of the Old English word ''hālig'' (holy, sacred; healthy, sound)<ref>[https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/halig#Old_English Hāliġ] in Wiktionary</ref><ref>[https://bosworthtoller.com/18045 Hālig] in Bosworth-Toller Anglo-Saxon Dictionary</ref> and ''tree''. A literal translation would be "holy tree".
* In [[Unused Content|cut]] dialogue lines, [[Old Albus]] referenced a chalk city of Miquella called ''"Eburion"''.<ref>[[Old Albus]]: ''"<!--A chosen land awaits us Albinaurics. -->Miquella's city, far to the north, a place called Eburion, where we are conferred eternal grace.<!-- The medallion is the key that leads to the city. It's only a quaint treasure, for we who cannot make the journey. But for dear Latenna, it is needed. To fulfil her purpose.-->"''</ref><ref>[[Old Albus]]: ''"<!--My legs will soon fade, and with them, my life. Alas, this is the immovable fate of all Albinaurics. -->If only I could have gazed upon it, even once. The great chalk buttress of Eburion.''"</ref> This area may have been reworked into Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.<ref name="elphael"/>
