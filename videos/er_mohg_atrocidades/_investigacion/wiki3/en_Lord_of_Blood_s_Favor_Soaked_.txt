The '''Lord of Blood's Favor''' is a [[Key Items|Key Item]] in {{ER}}.

 | EN_line2 = The final trial is complete. [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Luminary Mohg]] is sure to welcome you into his service, as a [[Pureblood Knights|knight]] who will lay the foundations for his [[Mohgwyn Dynasty|dynasty]].

==Effects==
* Present the soaked Lord of Blood's Favor to [[White Mask Varré]] and select '''Anoint Me''' to receive the [[Bloody Finger]].
* Speak to him again to obtain the [[Pureblood Knight's Medal]], which grants access to [[Mohgwyn Palace]].

==Acquisition==
'''Location''': [[Rose Church]], [[Liurnia of the Lakes]]
* Obtained by soaking the [[Lord of Blood's Favor]] in a [[Finger Maidens|maiden's]] blood. This is done during White Mask Varré's questline by choosing one of the following options:
# Interact with the corpse of [[The Tarnished (Protagonist)|the Tarnished]]'s own [[Finger Maiden]] at the [[Chapel of Anticipation]] (requires an [[Imbued Sword Key]] to access the [[Sending Gate]] at [[The Four Belfries]]).
# Interact with the corpse of [[Vyke's Finger Maiden]] at the [[Church of Inhibition]] near the {{grace}} [[Frenzied Flame Village Outskirts]].
# Kill [[Irina of Morne]] at the [[Bridge of Sacrifice]] in the [[Weeping Peninsula]], or, if already deceased, kill [[Lightseeker Hyetta]] at any of her locations.

==Notes==
* This blood-drenched Lord of Blood's Favor displays an emblem associated with the [[Mohgwyn Dynasty]].
* The ritual of soaking the favor in a sacrificial maiden’s blood is an initiation rite for the [[Pureblood Knights]].<ref>[[White Mask Varré]]: ''"Now, take this. For your final trial. Soak the cloth with a maiden's blood. Normally, this ritual would involve killing one's own maiden, and recanting the wisdom of the Two Fingers. But since you are maidenless, the blood of anyone's maiden will do."''</ref>
