'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[NPC]] in {{ER}}.

==Overview==
Magnus the Beast Claw is an NPC that can be invaded. To invade him, one must go to the [[Writheblood Ruins]] and find an invading summon sign, which costs one [[Festering Bloody Finger]] each try to activate. Once the sign is activated, the player will invade Magnus' world with the objective of killing him. The main point of this NPC is  to have another option to advance in [[White Mask Varré]]'s questline, where the Tarnished is tasked to try invasions 3 times, of either him or other players (though defeating him also counts as all 3 invasions).

Though there are few, if any, hints as to his place in the world, under his mask his face has vertical red markings similar to those borne by the [[Ancestral Follower Warrior]]s.

==Strategy==
*Both [[Beast Claw (incantation)|Beast Claw]] and [[Gurranq's Beast Claw]] can be jumped over. 
**This means they serve as good openings for jumping attacks, should you be ready to approach as he starts them up. 
*[[Bestial Sling]] can simply be dodged through straight forwards and he's unlikely to spam it, limiting its power. 
*Having both a [[Great Hammers|Great Hammer]] (one with [[Skill: Lion's Claw|Lion's Claw]] as its skill at that) makes it rather unsafe to block him, especially with anything that isn't at least a decent [[Greatshields|Greatshield]]. 

==Trivia==
*Added with [[Game Version/1.06|Patch 1.06]]. 
*The summon sign used for the invasion only disappears once he is killed, even if [[White Mask Varré|White Mask Varré's]] quest line progresses past this point.
