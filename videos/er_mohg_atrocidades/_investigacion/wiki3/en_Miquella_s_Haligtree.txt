'''Miquella's Haligtree''' (ミケラの聖樹, ''Mikera no Seiju'') is a [[legacy dungeon]] located north of the [[Consecrated Snowfield]]. It is accessible after solving the puzzle in the [[Evergaol]] at [[Ordina, Liturgical Town]] and taking the [[Sending Gate]].

==Overview==
[[File:ER Legacy Dungeon Map Miquella's Haligtree.jpg|thumb|Map of the Haligtree]]
[[Miquella the Kind|Miquella]]'s Haligtree is a vast, misshapen tree in the far north of the [[Lands Between]].<ref name="greatshield">[[Haligtree Crest Greatshield]]</ref> Built into the canopy is [[Haligtree Town]], a sanctuary for the unwanted. At the base of the tree is [[Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree]], a city largely inhabited by the [[Haligtree Knight]]s and [[Cleanrot Knight]]s who serve Miquella and [[Malenia, Blade of Miquella|Malenia]]. Both areas contain statues of the twin [[Demigods]].

Long ago, the [[Empyreans|Empyrean]] Miquella abandoned [[Golden Order]] Fundamentalism after realizing it could not treat [[Malenia, Blade of Miquella|Malenia]]'s [[Scarlet Rot]].<ref>[[Radagon's Rings of Light]]</ref> He sought to establish an [[Unalloyed Gold]] order away from the [[Erdtree]] and settled on the Haligtree.<ref name="greatshield"/> Miquella watered the sapling with his own Empyrean blood, and later wove a cocoon and embedded himself within its trunk to nurture it directly.<ref>[[Haligtree Knight Armor]]</ref> However, it would fail to grow into an [[Erdtree]]. Before he could finish, Miquella was cut and taken from the tree by [[Mohg, Lord of Blood|Mohg]],<ref>[[Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing]]: ''"I heard speculation Miquella embedded himself in the Haligtree, but before he could finish, someone cut the tree open and absconded with his infant form."''</ref> who was under his charm. The Lord of Blood zealously wished to raise the Empyrean to [[Gods|godhood]] and become his consort.<ref>[[Remembrance of the Blood Lord]]</ref> After returning from her battle with [[Radahn]], Malenia awaited her brother's return at the base of the tree,<ref>[[Malenia's Winged Helm]]</ref> and over time infected the tree with her Scarlet Rot.

Nevertheless, the Haligtree and its city became a haven for those rejected by society, such as the [[Misbegotten]] and the [[Kindred of Rot]]. [[Loretta]], a former [[Carian Knight|Knight of Caria]], founded a sanctuary for [[Albinaurics]] among the boughs of the Haligtree.<ref>[[Old Albus]]: ''"A chosen land awaits us Albinaurics. The medallion is the key that leads to the city. It's only a quaint treasure, for we who cannot make the journey. But for dear Latenna, it is needed. To fulfil her purpose."''</ref><ref>[[Royal Knight Helm]]</ref> Those who made the pilgrimage presented a [[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right)|secret medallion]] at the [[Grand Lift of Rold]] to gain access to the Consecrated Snowfield. They traveled north and crossed the river to Ordina, Liturgical Town, where they journeyed through a Sending Gate.<ref>[[Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left)]]</ref><ref>[[Map Consecrated Snowfield]]</ref>

==Notes==
* The Haligtree appears to have grown from the stump of an older tree.

==Trivia==
* Haligtree is a combination of the Old English word ''hālig'' (holy, sacred; healthy, sound)<ref>[https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/halig#Old_English Hāliġ] in Wiktionary</ref><ref>[https://bosworthtoller.com/18045 Hālig] in Bosworth-Toller Anglo-Saxon Dictionary</ref> and ''tree''. A literal translation would be "holy tree".
