==Trivia==
*The weapon's Japanese name, '''''Shizanketsuga''''' (屍山血河), is a four-character idiom referring to mounds of corpses and "rivers" of blood left in the aftermath of a battle.<ref>[https://jisho.org/word/屍山血河 Jisho] entry for 屍山血河</ref>
**Similarly, Rivers of Blood's Unique Skill is called ''Shishiruirui'' (死屍累々) in Japanese, another idiom that also refers to heaps of corpses left on a battlefield.<ref>[https://jisho.org/word/死屍累々 Jisho] entry for 死屍累々</ref>
