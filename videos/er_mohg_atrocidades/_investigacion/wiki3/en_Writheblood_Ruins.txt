The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[location]] in {{ER}}, located on the [[Altus Plateau]].

==Description==
A set of ruins in northern [[Altus Plateau]], overrun by creatures infected by cursed blood.

A red summon sign can be found in the north-eastern building, which can be used to invade the world of [[Magnus the Beast Claw]]. A [[Bloody Helice]] loot is protected by a [[Sanguine Noble]] mini-boss below the ruins.

The nearest graces is the [[Bridge of Iniquity]].
